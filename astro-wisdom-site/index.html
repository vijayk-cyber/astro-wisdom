  • Only one third-party service is named (Swiss Ephemeris). Maps, auth, payments, video, AI model, vectors, analytics and helpdesk are categories without vendors or costs.
  • No infrastructure, API, licence or team cost estimate, so there is no margin view. The cost model in this blueprint fills that gap.
  • No visual system, wireframes or motion specification — the UX guidance is principles only.
  • App-store billing is not addressed. Digital subscriptions in the apps carry 15–30% store commission, which changes pricing and margin.
  • Free-tier AI cost is not modelled. Unlimited free AI chat is the largest variable cost (about 22% of net revenue under these assumptions); a quota and a cheaper model for free users are needed.
  • India privacy law (DPDP Act) appears only as export/delete controls. A consent and retention document is needed before beta.
  • Offline behaviour, accessibility targets and Tamil/Hindi typography are not specified.
  • Benchmarks such as ≤1 arcsecond are proposed targets; the reference ephemeris and tolerance must be defined in the test corpus.
